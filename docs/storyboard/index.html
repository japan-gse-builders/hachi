2. 問題と出発点

All over the world, more older people live alone. Their families often live far away, and care staff cannot phone everyone every day. We are starting where that gap is widest: older English speakers in Japan, where local services speak Japanese. Many will never use an app; the device by their chair is a landline. So Hachi needs nothing but a telephone.

  • 前半は住宅街や固定電話のある部屋の映像。
  • 「older English speakers in Japan」で「Starting in Japan」のカード。

3. 毎日の電話

Every day, Hachi calls. It says it is an AI and that the call is recorded. Then it simply chats.

通話音声 9 秒：Edith が睡眠について答える。「Whole conversation」を開いておき、文字起こしも映す。

Health and meals come up naturally, with two short screening questions. Hachi listens to what people say, and to how they say it.

  • 字幕：All people shown are fictional.
  • 字幕：Calls start only after the person agrees, and staff record that agreement.

4. 職員の待ち行列

Staff start from one queue that says who to look at first, and why. The queue also shows who Hachi could not reach, so a quiet day is never mistaken for a good one.

  • レーンを上から下へ：Immediate action → Review today → Review soon → Monitor。
  • 「Review soon」の Edith Carter のカードで止めて、押してケース画面へ。
  • 「Filter cases」は撮影前に閉じておく（広い画面では開いた状態で表示される）。

プレビューには「Nobody has been reached」の枠が出ていない。本番で枠が出ていない日は、2 文目は画面なしで読む。

5. Edith の小さな変化（R3）

Edith is marked "Review soon". Nothing she said was alarming. But listen.

証拠の音声 3 秒：「…from the from the bakery…」（1:15 in）

She is restarting her sentences more often than she usually does. Hachi compares her only with herself, over the past four weeks. Not a diagnosis: a sign of change, with the recording as evidence.

  • 「Speech over time」の札 → 指標ごとの札 →「Usual for Edith」の帯から外れた最新の点（ひし形）。
  • 最新の点にカーソルを置いて「(usual … to …)」の吹き出しを出す。

このプレビューでは「Time to answer」のグラフが先に開いている。本番でも、最初に開くグラフと 2 文目の言い方を合わせる。

6. 緊急（R1）と沈黙

If someone is in danger now, like chest pain or a fall, Hachi acts during the call. It asks them to call an ambulance if they can, and phones staff and family to join the call. Picking up and saying nothing is noticed too.

  • 赤い「Immediate action」の札と「Possible immediate danger to life or health. Act now.」
  • 本人の発言 →「Who was told」で職員と家族に届いた記録。
  • 字幕：Hedged wording counts. Past events, other people and denials do not.

要決定：本番ではこの場面に映すケースがまだない。推奨は合成の緊急デモ利用者を 1 人追加すること（この画像はその完成イメージ）。

7. 家族の画面

Family get a simpler view: today's call, two weeks at a glance, and what keeps coming up. They never get recordings, transcripts or speech analysis, so Edith can talk freely. The API enforces that.

  • 携帯の画面幅で撮る。状態の枠 → 今日の通話のタイル → 14 日グリッド →「What keeps coming up」。
  • 職員画面にあった話し方のグラフや録音が、ここには無いことが伝わる順に見せる。

8. 仕組み

Twilio streams the call through Cloud Run to the Gemini Live API, and Gemini reviews every call afterwards. A monthly AI budget can pause routine calls, never an emergency alert.

  • サービス名を順に軽くハイライト。
  • 最後の一文で docs/COST_CONTROL.md の「二つの上限の順序」の表へ。

9. 評価とこれから

On synthetic recordings, Hachi caught all ten scripted changes with no false alarms; real phone audio will be harder. Today, Hachi calls English speakers in Japan. Next, English speakers anywhere. All they need is a phone.

  • 評価カード → 締めのカードと URL。
  • 世界展開は「次にやること」として、ここで一度だけ言う。
