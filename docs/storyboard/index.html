2. 世界の問題と出発点

Around the world, more older people live alone. Family is often far away, and care staff cannot call everyone every day. Many will never use an app, but they still answer the phone. We are starting with English speakers in Japan, one of the oldest societies in the world.

  • 前半は一人で暮らす高齢者の部屋と電話の映像。国が分かる看板や文字は映さない。

4. 職員の待ち行列

Staff start from one queue that says who to look at first, and why. It also shows who Hachi could not reach, so a missed call never looks like a good day.

  • レーンを上から下へ：Immediate action → Review today → Review soon → Monitor。
  • 「Review soon」の Edith Carter のカードで止めて、押してケース画面へ。
  • 「Filter cases」は撮影前に閉じておく（広い画面では開いた状態で表示される）。

プレビューには「Nobody has been reached」の枠が出ていない。本番で枠が出ていない日は、2 文目は画面なしで読む。

5. Edith の小さな変化（R3）

Nothing Edith said was alarming. But listen.

証拠の音声 3 秒：「…from the from the bakery…」（1:15 in）

She is slower to answer than usual, and restarts her sentences more often. Hachi compares her only with herself, over the past four weeks. Not a diagnosis: a sign of change, with the recording as evidence.

  • 「Speech over time」の札 → 指標ごとの札 →「Usual for Edith」の帯から外れた最新の点（ひし形）。
  • 最新の点にカーソルを置いて「(usual … to …)」の吹き出しを出す。

Edith の台本の変化は答えるまでの時間（評価では Time to answer と Stops and restarts が変化）。本番でどちらかが変化を示していなければ、2 文目のその半分を削る。

6. 緊急（R1）と沈黙（R4）

If someone is in danger now, like chest pain or a fall, Hachi acts during the call. It asks them to call an ambulance if they can, and phones staff and family to join the call. Answering and saying nothing raises an alert too.

  • 赤い「Immediate action」の札と「Possible immediate danger to life or health. Act now.」
  • 本人の発言 →「Who was told」で職員と家族に届いた記録。
  • 字幕："It's probably nothing, but my chest hurts" still counts. Past events and other people's problems do not.

要決定：本番ではこの場面に映すケースがまだない。推奨は合成の緊急デモ利用者を 1 人追加すること（この画像はその完成イメージ）。

7. 家族の画面

Families get a simpler view: today's call, two weeks at a glance, and what keeps coming up. They never get recordings, transcripts or speech analysis, so Edith can talk freely. The API enforces that.

  • 携帯の画面幅で撮る。今日の通話のタイル → 14 日グリッド →「What keeps coming up」。上部の「Daily calls are paused」は映さない。

8. 仕組み

Twilio streams the call through Cloud Run to the Gemini Live API, and Gemini reviews every call afterwards. A monthly AI budget can pause routine calls, but never an emergency alert.

9. 評価とこれから

On synthetic recordings, Hachi found all ten scripted changes, with no false alarms. Real phone audio will be harder. Today, Hachi calls English speakers in Japan. Next, English speakers anywhere. All they need is a phone.
